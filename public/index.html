FIT NOTE

These are general reference measurements, not manufacturer-verified specs. Garment fit can vary by product and supplier; check a product-specific chart when available. For best results, compare these measurements with a similar garment you already own.

HOW TO MEASURE
CHEST WIDTH
Lay the garment flat and measure straight across from one armpit seam to the other. This is width, not body circumference.
BODY LENGTH
Measure from the highest shoulder point beside the collar to the bottom hem.
SHOULDER
Measure straight across the back between the shoulder seams.
HOODIE SLEEVE
Measure from the center back of the neck, across the shoulder, to the sleeve cuff.
